FAQ

Planning questions.

How far ahead should we book?

3–6 months for weddings, 4–8 weeks for birthdays and corporate events. December weekends usually fill by October.

Do you work outside Lagos and Abuja?

Yes. We travel across Nigeria; logistics are quoted separately.

How do payments work?

50% to secure your date, 40% two weeks before, and 10% after the event.

Can we see the design before the day?

Yes. Signature and Grand include a mood board and a styled mock-up table.
