FAQ

Good to know.

How early should I order?

48 hours for custom cakes, 24 hours for trays, and same day for small chops before 12 noon.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer or card link. A 50% deposit secures your date, and the balance is due before delivery.

Can you match a design I saw online?

Yes. Send the picture with your order and we'll confirm what's possible and the price.

Do you do eggless or sugar-reduced cakes?

Yes, on request, for most flavours.
