FAQ

Before you book.

How long does a bespoke piece take?

14 working days for most pieces, 4–8 weeks for bridal. Rush orders are possible at an extra fee.

Do I need to bring my own fabric?

You can, or choose from our curated fabrics in studio. Fabric is quoted separately.

How does payment work?

70% deposit to begin, balance at the final fitting. Bank transfer or card.

Can you make for clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. We fit over video call with our measurement guide and ship with DHL.
